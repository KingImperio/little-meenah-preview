# Mood artwork

Emoji icons designed by Md Tanvirul Haque from Flaticon. Free PNG use with visible attribution, included beneath the mood carousel.

Creator: https://www.flaticon.com/authors/md-tanvirul-haque
License: https://www.flaticon.com/license/license.pdf

All sixteen icons come from a single illustrated emoji series so the carousel reads as one set. Each mood has its own image; no icon is reused for two moods.
